Sequence Syntax

Connected 1 die rolls. The lower results are with 1 and 2 rerolls.

  • 2+ means a normal D6 roll succeeding on 2 or higher.
  • 3++ means a 3+ roll with one built-in reroll for that die.
  • You can leave out whitespace and + entirely, for example 2+3++4+ and 23++4 means 2+ 3++ 4+.
  • p anywhere (e.g. 2+ p 4+ or 3+p) or the Pro key gives the whole sequence one Pro reroll (2/3 chance).

Block dice:

  • 1d,2d,3d,-2d,-3d success on pow/pow*
  • b anywhere (or the Brawler toggle) gives every block Brawler: re-roll one Both Down die.
  • Add + (Block, or use the toggle), - (No turnover: push or better), * (Pow only), / (Push only: exactly a push)
  • a9 or av9 is a 2D6 armor-break check.
  • k (KO) or i (Injury/CAS) after an armour roll adds the injury roll, e.g. av9k. Results always list Break / KO / CAS.
  • s anywhere except right after a D6 digit (or the vs Stunty toggle) uses the Stunty injury table (+1) and turns a Pow* into a Push. m after armour (or the MB toggle) is Mighty Blow.
Example: 2+ 4+ 2d+ av9im for an Ogre with Block to activate, dodge & injure a lone Human Lineman

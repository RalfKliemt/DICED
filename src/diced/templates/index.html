Sequence Syntax

Connected 1 die rolls. The lower results are with 1 and 2 rerolls.

  • 2+ means a normal D6 roll succeeding on 2 or higher.
  • 3++ means a 3+ roll with one built-in reroll for that die.
  • You can leave out whitespace and + entirely, for example 2+3++4+ and 23++4 means 2+ 3++ 4+.
  • p or 3+p adds a 2/3 reroll chance on that die.

Block dice:

  • 1d,2d,3d,-2d,-3d success on pow/pow*
  • Add + (Block), - (Push), * (Pow only), / (Push only)
  • a9 or av9 is a 2D6 armor-break check.
  • Injury suffixes: k (KO), i (Injury), s (Stunty), and m (Mighty Blow) can follow armor rolls.
Example: 2+ 4+ 12d+ av9im for an Ogre with Block to activate, dodge & injure a Human Lineman

Sequence Syntax

Every roll in the chain must succeed. The smaller numbers are with 1 and 2 team rerolls.

  • 2+ means a D6 roll succeeding on 2 or higher.
  • 3++ means a 3+ roll with a skill reroll for that die.
  • Whitespace and + can be left out: 2+3++4+ and 23++4 mean 2+ 3++ 4+.
  • p anywhere (or the Pro toggle) gives the whole sequence one Pro reroll.

Block dice:

  • 1d, 2d, 3d, -2d, -3d succeed on a knock-down result.
  • Add - (No turnover: push or better), * (Pow only), / (Push only).
  • b anywhere (or the Brawler toggle) lets every block re-roll one Both Down die.
  • The Block toggle (or + after a block) counts Both Down as a success.

Armour:

  • av9 is a 2D6 armour roll needing 9+.
  • k (KO) or i (casualty) adds the injury roll, e.g. av9k. Results always list Break / KO / CAS.
  • s anywhere except right after a D6 digit (or vs Stunty) uses the Stunty injury table (+1) and turns a Pow* into a Push; m (or MB) is Mighty Blow.

Example: 2+ 4+ 2d av9i with Block and MB on.
